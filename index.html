Five floors. Each theme teaches a different hazard (runes, vents, icicles, wisps, spores). Floating door-runes tell you what a room holds: gold treasure · violet elite · blue shrine · red the boss. Clearing a room's foes pays a gold bonus. Side goals — maze prize, shrine, explore, theme lesson — pay gold without blocking the boss. Level-ups wait until the room is clear. Warm orange chests can bite.
