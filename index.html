Conquer all five floors. Each boss lair is warded — slay foes to shatter the seal, then fell the boss to open the portal (and its shop) down. Red grunts swarm, blue casters snipe, orange chargers ram; spike traps skewer anything standing on them, and frozen lakes are treacherously slick. Dash (Shift) through danger, spend gold at the Wayshop, and claim boons at shrines — the Scrying Orb relic even lets F lift the fog. The Tyrant waits on floor five.
